[ Site upgrade in progress, 09/15/2023 ] I'm pursuing my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University. I also acquired my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is in Reinforcement Learning (RL), Robotics, Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), and Computational Design. I am priviledged to be co-advised by two amazing advisors, Dr. Daniel Cardoso Llach (Computational Design) and Dr. Jean Oh (Robotics). If you ask me what drives my research, it is those touchy moments when robots show subtle understanding and consideration of humans. I can still remember the evening when I was working late in the lab, pacing before the whiteboard, and thinking anxiously about a technical issue. Husky, the robot I built, was navigating around the lab. When approaching behind me, the robot gently slowed down, quietly turned around, and took a short detour, as if it was careful not to interrupt. This behavior was enacted by a trained RL model and would not have occurred if the robot had been programmed purely for goal-reaching efficiency purposes. Although still at a preliminary stage, I think I found my passion for human-robot interaction (HRI), robot learning and human-centered AI that night.

Currently, with interdisciplinary knowledge in machine learning, robotics, and computational design, I wish to leverage recent AI and robotics advances to humanely and sensitively support people in their existing labor-intensive work. To achieve this goal, I am designing and developing socially embodied robotic prototypes, frameworks, and RL algorithms that can incrementally realize this vision. As an example, an important part of my Ph.D. research is to prototype an RL-driven companion robot that can safely and comfortably assist and accompany on-site construction workers by providing handy support.

Prior to CMU, I obtained my M.Arch. and B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. In May 2018, I co-founded a startup (Metascope Tech) that aimed to provide B2B consultation to the Chinese commercial architecture market. By applying machine learning and computer vision techniques to an aesthetic recommendation system, we profile the spatial preferences of clients to assist architects in making better design decisions. During my leisure time, I enjoy photography, cooking, reading, playing drums, and taking care of animals/plants. I am the (proud) cat parent of two extremely cute little beasts: Sophie (Tangtang) and Huihui. As for my favorite readings, one of them is Herbert Simon's The Sciences of the Artificial. He was also the reason I chose Carnegie Mellon for my doctoral studies. If you are interested in the other side of me: SIDE-B: IMMATURE ARCHITECT OF THINGS, go ahead and flip it.

I'm pursuing my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University. I also acquired my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is in Reinforcement Learning (RL), Robotics, and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). I am priviledged to be co-advised by two amazing advisors, Dr. Daniel Cardoso Llach (Computational Design) and Dr. Jean Oh (Robotics). With interdisciplinary knowledge in machine learning, robotics, computational design, and architecture, I’m constantly motivated by the vision of AI helping us build a smart and graceful future. Coming from an architecture background and witnessing first-hand the intensity of manual labor in traditional industries, at the moment I aim to leverage AI and robotics to approach the problem of automation in a humane, contextualized fashion. More specifically, instead of designing robots to fully replace human, I prefer robots that can support, assist, and accompany human in existing labor-intensive workflows. To live up to this goal, I'm currently working on building RL-driven robotic frameworks and prototypes that can collaboratively support human workers in highly manual construction environments.

Prior to CMU, I obtained my M.Arch. and B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. In May 2018, I co-founded a startup (Metascope Tech) that aims at providing B2B consultation to Chinese commercial architecture market. By applying machine learning and computer vision techniques to an aesthetics recommendation system, we profile clients' spatial preferences to assist architects form better design decisions. In my leisure time, I enjoy photography, cooking, reading, and drum playing. I'm also the cat parent of two extremely cute little beasts: Sophie (Tangtang) and Huihui. If you are curious about my favorite readings, one of them is Herbert Simon's The Sciences of the Artificial. He is also the reason why I choose Carnegie Mellon for my doctoral studies.

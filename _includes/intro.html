I'm pursuing my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University. I also acquired my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is in Reinforcement Learning (RL), Robotics, and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). With an interdisciplinary background in machine learning, robotics, computational design, and architecture, I’m constantly motivated by the vision of AI helping us build smarter, both virtually and concretely. Coming from an architecture background and witnessing first-hand the intensity of manual labor in traditional industries, I aim to leverage AI and robotics to approach the problem of automation in a humane fashion. To live up to this goal, I'm currently working on building robotic prototypes and frameworks driven by SoTA AI methods that can support human workers in their existing work environments.

Prior to CMU, I obtained my M.Arch. and B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. In May 2018, I co-founded a startup (Metascope Tech) that aims at providing B2B consultation to Chinese commercial architecture market. By applying machine learning and computer vision techniques to an aesthetics recommendation system, we profile clients' spatial preferences to assist architects form better design decisions.

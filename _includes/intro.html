I'm pursuing my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University. I also acquired my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is in Reinforcement Learning (RL), Robotics, and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). I am priviledged to be advised by two amazing advisors, Prof. Daniel Cardoso Llach (Computational Design) and Prof. Jean Oh (Robotics). With interdisciplinary training in machine learning, robotics, computational design, and architecture, I’m constantly motivated by the vision of AI helping us build a smart and graceful future. Coming from an architecture background and witnessing first-hand the intensity of manual labor in traditional industries, I aim to leverage AI and robotics to approach the problem of automation in a humane fashion. More specifically, instead of designing robots to fully replace human, I prefer robots that can support, assist, and accompany human in existing workflows. To live up to this goal, I'm currently working on building RL-driven robotic frameworks and prototypes that can collaboratively support human workers in labor-intensive construction environments.

Prior to CMU, I obtained my M.Arch. and B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. In May 2018, I co-founded a startup (Metascope Tech) that aims at providing B2B consultation to Chinese commercial architecture market. By applying machine learning and computer vision techniques to an aesthetics recommendation system, we profile clients' spatial preferences to assist architects form better design decisions.

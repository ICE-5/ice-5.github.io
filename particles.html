PARTICLES LAB ｜ EXP-1

Fluidity vs. Stability: Shaping Dynamic Macrostructure Within Diffusive Flows

This series of particle systems is designed to visualize my sparks, inspirations, ideas, and computational thinking in AI/ML research. In this experiment, EXP-1, a seemingly continuous flow emerges from the collective behavior of homogeneous discrete particles. The experiment explores how certain universal principles can shape—or “tame”—a highly diffusive system into a temporarily semi-stable state with subtle macrostructures.

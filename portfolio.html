Welcome to SIDE-B.

As a designer and creator at heart, my curiosity spans across disciplines, driven by a fascination with crafting elegant systems that shape both the physical and digital worlds—especially when they embody the art and science of designing elegant, functional, and intelligent entities. My journey of refining this architect and prototyper mindset began with the active design of physical spaces, forms and functions. This page showcases some of my earlier works that reflect this interconnected vision. While these projects primarily focus on spatial storytelling and innovation, they explore unbounded ideas and expressive concepts. For a look at my more recent research in AI/ML and robotics, feel free to return to SIDE-A: AI+ROBOTICS RESEARCHER.

ARCHITECT OF SYSTEMS AND SPACES

Welcome to SIDE-B.

I’ve always been a designer and architect at heart—drawn to the structure of ideas, the elegance of form, and the purpose behind systems. My journey began in the world of spatial design, but it was also where my path began to diverge. I realized I wasn’t content with creating static structures—I was drawn to things that think, move, and respond. Whether physical or computational, I’ve long been driven by the art and science of designing systems that are aesthetically resonant and intelligent.

Along the way, I developed two instincts that continue to shape how I think and build: (1) an ability to design forms in service of abstract or complex functions, and (2) an aesthetic intuition that bridges technical and creative domains.

Herbert Simon once wrote, “The proper study of mankind is the science of design,” and, “Artificial intelligence is no more or less than the study of design itself.” These words didn’t just resonate—they named a path I was already walking. That path led to SIDE-A: AI/ML RESEARCHER.

This page gathers fragments of my earlier work in spatial storytelling. They are speculative, expressive, and at times unfinished—but each carries the spirit of inquiry I’ve never let go. I’ve intentionally left out polished renderings in favor of dry clarity—letting the core ideas stand on their own.

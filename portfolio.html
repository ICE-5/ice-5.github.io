ARCHITECTURE OF THINGS

•

Welcome to SIDE-B. As a curious person who has always appreciated (all kinds of) elegant systems emerged in the flow of human civilization and beyond, my journey starts in architecture. Although people might find it confusing how someone trained to design buildings ended up working with robots and AI, to me they share the same philosophical traits of architecting amazing, beautiful, and eventually intelligent things. This page contains some of the initial seeds hinting at this intention. These immature works, though all taking buildings and spaces as agencies, are largely unbounded, divergent, and expressive of different thoughts. Of course, please feel free to go back to SIDE-A: AI+robotics researcher.

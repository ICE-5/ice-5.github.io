/ ABOUT ME

I'm a Ph.D. student at  Carnegie Mellon University . I've also earned my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is mainly in robotics, reinforcement learning, and collective robotic construction. I place my investigation at teaching robots to learn smarter skills related to building elegant and artistic physical creations. Besides that, I enjoy experiencing every aspect of design and making, partly because of my interdisciplinary background in architecture and mechanical engineering. Prior to CMU, I obtained my M.Arch. and B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. Since May 2018, I've co-founded a startup (Metascope Tech) that aims at providing insightful B2B consultation to Chinese commercial architecture design market by active user profiling and data analysis with large-scale machine learning. Though no longer serving as its CEO, the company is still in rapid development. In my leisure time, I enjoy photography, cooking, reading, and drum playing. I can be reached at yuningw [at] cs [dot] cmu [dot] edu .

About Me

I'm a Ph.D. student at  Carnegie Mellon University . I've also earned my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is mainly in robotics, reinforcement learning, multi-agent system, and collective robotic construction. Besides that, I enjoy experiencing every aspects of design, creation and hands-on making, partly because of my interdisciplinary background in architecture and mechanical engineering. I can be reached at yuningw [at] cs [dot] cmu [dot] edu .

ABOUT ME

•

I'm a Ph.D. student at Carnegie Mellon University . I've also earned my M.S. in Machine Learning here. My research interest is in reinforcement learning, robotics, human-robot interaction . With an interdisciplinary background in machine learning, robotics, and computational design, I’m constantly motivated by the vision of AI helping us build a better cyber-future, both virtually and concretely. I enjoy prototyping and making things from 0 to 1. I enjoy exploring solutions to critical problems. I’m a do-er . Prior to CMU, I obtained my M.Arch. and B.Arch. from Tsinghua University in Beijing, China. Since May 2018, I've co-founded a startup (Metascope Tech) that aims at providing B2B consultation to Chinese commercial architecture design market. Applying ML and CV to an aesthetics recommender, we aim to profile clients' spatial preferences to facilitate architects form better design decisions. In my leisure time, I enjoy photography, cooking, reading, and drum playing. I can be reached at yuningw [at] cs [dot] cmu [dot] edu.

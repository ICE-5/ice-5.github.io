About Me

I'm a first-year Ph.D. CD, M.S. ML student at Carnegie Mellon University. My research interest is mainly in multi-agent robotic system, robotic learning, reinforcement learning and collective robotic construction. Constantly fascinated by elegant system architectures, I long for creating intelligent robot "society" with various hierarchical organization, collective behaviour, seamless local / global communication, and interaction with human.

Besides that, I enjoy experiencing every aspects of design, creation and construction, partly because of my background in architecture, mechanical engineering and digital fabrication. For more information about my bio/CV, research, projects and blogs, feel free to explore this site!

I can be reached at yuningw[at]andrew[dot]cmu[dot]edu.
